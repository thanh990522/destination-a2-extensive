# Source register

Checked on 14 November 2026.

## Supplied book

**English Exam Preparation: Grammar and Vocabulary, Level A2** (Macmillan, eds. Maria Verbitskaya, Malcolm Mann, Steve Taylore-Knowles), supplied as `Des_A2_English_Print_Clear.pdf`. The PDF is not part of this repository.

- Units 1–6 were inspected page by page: Unit 1 (pages 6–8), Unit 2 (pages 9–11), Unit 3 (pages 12–15), Unit 4 (pages 18–20), Unit 5 (pages 21–23), Unit 6 (pages 24–27).
- Grammar scope (forms, uses, helpful hints, watch-out notes, the stative-verb list) and the Unit 3 word lists by category follow the book.
- **Units 1–6** follow the teacher’s instruction to use the book directly: the grammar theory and word lists (with Vietnamese explanations added) and the book exercises are **quoted exactly**, including instructions and word boxes: Unit 1 A–F (pages 7–8), Unit 2 A–F (pages 10–11), Unit 3 A–I (pages 13–15), Unit 4 A–F (pages 19–20), Unit 5 A–F (pages 22–23), Unit 6 A–I (pages 25–27). Two adaptations for the screen: Unit 3 exercise F (crossword) is shown as its numbered Across/Down clues without the grid, and Unit 3 exercise D (matching) is shown as two tap-to-match columns in the book’s order. The instructions of Unit 3 H and I read “grammatically and in meaning” as printed. Stray scanning marks before some instructions (“EH”, “DD”, “iF |”, “a”, “(D)”) are left out. Unit 6 A keeps its three word boxes, each above its own three sentences; Unit 6 G shows each anagram under its gap. The answer keys were written for this course (the book pages have no key).
- All reading texts, skill tasks, writing models and homework are newly written.
- The book is copyrighted by its publisher. The site is public on GitHub Pages: keep the copied book material for students who use the book, and consider closing lessons in the admin page when they are not in use.

## Real recordings (all from ELLLO, English Listening Lesson Library Online, elllo.org)

Each MP3 endpoint was downloaded and decoded (duration checked) and each question key was checked against the script on the lesson page. Audio streams from ELLLO and is not stored here; full scripts stay on the source pages. ELLLO refuses requests that carry another site’s Referer (HTTP 403, checked 14 November 2026), so the lesson, slide and homework pages send no referrer (`<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer">`); playback was then confirmed in Chrome for every recording.

## Design, icons and fonts

- Design reference: the user’s own course <https://thanh990522.github.io/communication-foundation/> (blue and yellow identity, admin with GitHub-API open/close, Google Apps Script progress sheet, auto-checked homework with a pass mark).
- Icons and illustrations: **Microsoft Fluent Emoji 3D** (<https://github.com/microsoft/fluentui-emoji>, MIT licence), resized to WebP by `tools/icons.py`. Emoji without a Fluent image fall back to the system emoji.
- Font: Be Vietnam Pro (Google Fonts, SIL Open Font Licence).
- Word pronunciation uses the browser’s built-in speech voice; it is not a recording.
